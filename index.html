Mental Health Juneteenth Resources

Explore resources dedicated to supporting the mental health and well-being of the Black community, especially in the context of Juneteenth:

Black Mental Health Alliance (BMHA)

Advocates for mental health and well-being of Black communities.

Therapy for Black Girls

Online platforms connecting Black individuals with culturally competent therapists.

The Loveland Foundation

Provides financial assistance for Black women and girls seeking therapy.

Mental Health America (MHA) - Black & African American Communities

Offers resources and information specific to the Black community.

NAMI (National Alliance on Mental Illness) - African American Mental Health

Provides support, education, and advocacy.

Juneteenth Educational Sites

Many sites offer historical context which can be vital for communal healing.
